About the website

Our mission

NorSnad Labs is an educational initiative focused on making NorSand more accessible. Some NorSand concepts such as the state parameter, image point, internal cap, limiting yield surface, dilatancy, and yield-surface evolution can be difficult to understand from equations and static diagrams alone. Here, these concepts are explained gradually and connected to interactive visualizations of conventional triaxial tests. The purpose of this website is to make these ideas easier to learn.

The website is organized around three goals:

  • Learn the basic ideas of Critical State Soil Mechanics and NorSand and to provide a jumping off point for further education.
  • Visualize how stress, volume, state, dilatancy, and the NorSand yield surface evolve during loading by applying the model through validated examples
  • Aid in the application of NorSand through reproducible implementation and calibration resources.
NorSand Lab is intended to support continued research, collaboration, and development within the geotechnical engineering community.

Current scope and vision

The current version should be interpreted as a learning and research visualization tool for monotonic CID and CIU triaxial compression. It is not yet a general three-dimensional NorSand implementation. At this stage, the website does not cover arbitrary stress paths, principal-stress rotation, triaxial extension, plane-strain loading, cyclic loading, or general finite-element stress integration. These capabilities may be considered in future stages of the project.
