Mission

  1. Learn the basic ideas of Critical State Soil Mechanics and NorSand and to provide a jumping off point for further education.
  2. Visualize how stress, volume, state, dilatancy, and the NorSand yield surface evolve during loading by applying the model through validated examples
  3. Aid in the application of NorSand through reproducible implementation and calibration resources.

About the website

NorSand Labs is an educational initiative focused on making NorSand more accessible

Some NorSand concepts can be difficult to understand from equations and static diagrams alone. Here, these concepts are explained gradually and connected to interactive visualizations of conventional triaxial tests.

NorSand Lab is intended to support continued research, collaboration, and development within the geotechnical engineering community.

Current Scope and Vision

The current version should be interpreted as a learning and research visualization tool for monotonic CID and CIU triaxial compression.

It is not yet a general three-dimensional NorSand implementation. At this stage, the website does not cover arbitrary stress paths, principal-stress rotation, triaxial extension, plane-strain loading, cyclic loading, or general finite-element stress integration. These capabilities may be considered in future stages of the project.
