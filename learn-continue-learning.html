3. Interactive Simulations

3.4 Continue learning about NorSand

Readers new to NorSand may follow this learning path:

Effective stress → Critical state → Critical state line → State parameter → Dilatancy → Yield surface → Image point → Hardening and softening → Triaxial response

Readers can explore the material in different ways depending on their background. Those new to Critical State Soil Mechanics may begin with the introductory concepts and follow the suggested learning sequence, while readers already familiar with CSSM can proceed directly to the NorSand model components, validated triaxial examples, and interactive visualizations. The website is intended to connect NorSand theory with the response calculated during loading. Its explanations introduce the main concepts in accessible language, while the visualizations show how the stress state and internal variables evolve together. The objective is to help users understand not only the resulting stress–strain curves, but also the constitutive mechanisms that produce them. The material should be viewed as a guided learning resource rather than a complete derivation of NorSand. Readers seeking a rigorous treatment of the formulation, theoretical foundations, calibration, and broader applications should consult the original publications and the second edition of Soil Liquefaction: A Critical State Approach by Jefferies and Been.
