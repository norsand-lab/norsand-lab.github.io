2. Core concepts

2.11 Elastic stiffness

Before and during plastic loading, NorSand requires an elastic relationship. The formulation used for the simulations presented on this website adopts a pressure-dependent elastic shear modulusElastic stiffness controlling recoverable shear deformation:

G=Gref(p′pref)m

where Gref is the shear modulus at the reference pressure, G is the current shear modulus, m controls how stiffness changes with effective stress. For example, m=1 gives Gp′=constant, while m=0.5 gives G∝p′. A rigidity index may also be written as:

Ir=Gp′

It remains constant when m=1, but varies with mean effective stress for other values of m. The elastic parameters mainly influence the initial stiffness and the elastic part of the response, while the critical-state, dilatancy, and hardening parameters govern the subsequent elastoplastic response. Poisson's ratioElastic parameter that relates lateral strain to axial strain during elastic deformation, ν, or an equivalent bulk-modulus specification, is also required so that the elastic shear and volumetric responses are calculated consistently. The elastic bulk modulusElastic stiffness controlling recoverable volume change due to changes in mean stress is:

K=2G(1+ν)3(1−2ν)
