2. Core concepts

2.10 Hardening and softening

The NorSand hardening law controls the evolution of the image pressure pi′. When pi′<pi,max⁡′, plastic shearing generally causes the image pressure to increase and the yield surface to expand — this is referred to as hardening. When pi′>pi,max⁡′, the hardening law drives the image pressure downward, causing the yield surface to contract — this is referred to as softening. The model's hardening parameters control how pi′ evolves with plastic straining. At critical state, the state parameter and plastic dilatancy approach zero, and continued shearing occurs without further change in mean effective stress, deviator stress, or void ratio.
