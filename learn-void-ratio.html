2. Core concepts

2.3 Void ratio and soil packing

The packing of the soil is described using the void ratio,

e=VvVs

where Vv is the volume of voids and Vs is the volume of solids. A higher void ratio generally means that the particles are packed more loosely. A lower void ratio generally means that they are packed more densely. However, void ratio alone is not enough to predict soil behavior. The same void ratio can indicate a relatively dense state at one effective stress and a relatively loose state at another because the critical-state void ratio changes with mean effective stress. The density of the soil must therefore be interpreted together with its current effective stress. This is one of the central ideas behind the state parameter used by NorSand.
