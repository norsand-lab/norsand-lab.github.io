Resources

This page is a starting set of references for anyone who wants to go deeper into critical state soil mechanics and the NorSand model. It contains further reading material about concepts and technical details, software implementations of NorSand, and links to geotechnical professional societies.

Foundational papers

The core texts behind critical state soil mechanics and the NorSand formulation.

Been & Jefferies (1985), "A state parameter for sands" ↗

Géotechnique 35(2), 99–112

Introduces the state parameter ψ, a single measure combining void ratio and stress level relative to the critical state line to characterize sand behavior.

Been, Jefferies & Hachey (1991), "The critical state of sands" ↗

Géotechnique 41(3), 365–381

Examines a uniformly graded quartzitic sand to resolve conflicting evidence about whether "critical state" and "steady state" are the same thing, finding they coincide and are independent of stress path, sample preparation, and initial density.

Jefferies (1993), "Nor-Sand: a simple critical state model for sand" ↗

Géotechnique 43(1), 91–103

Develops NorSand, a generalized Cambridge-type constitutive model for sand from critical state axioms, using an "image point" on the yield surface tied to the state parameter to define hardening, instead of coupling yield surface size directly to void ratio.

Jefferies & Shuttle (2002), "Dilatancy in general Cambridge-type models" ↗

Géotechnique 52(9), 625–638

Shows that Cambridge-type models (Cam-clay, NorSand, etc.) produce inconsistent, sometimes physically implausible plastic strain rates when extended from triaxial to general 3D stress states, and fixes this with a work-conjugate strain measure plus interpolation between the triaxial compression/extension limits.

Jefferies & Shuttle (2011), "On the operating critical friction ratio in general stress states" ↗

Géotechnique 61(8), 709–713

Proposes a function for stress-dilatancy scaling based on an "operating" friction ratio, Mi, derived from work-conjugate stress and strain invariants rather than the final critical friction ratio alone. It shows this scaling coefficient evolves through loading and only reaches the true critical friction ratio at large strain.

Continue learning

Explains concepts behind NorSand and practical implementation. All except for the first one are available for free online

Jefferies, M. G., and Been, K. (2015). Soil Liquefaction: A Critical State Approach, 2nd ed ↗

CRC Press

Outlines a complete theoretical-to-practical pipeline for liquefaction engineering, structured around the state parameter ψ and the NorSand model. Comprehensive book about the theory and practice of NorSand

NorSand Model — Theory & Formulation ↗

docs.itascacg.com

Short derivation of the elasticity, yield surface, hardening rule, and state parameter. Also contains a glossary of model properties.

On the Calibration and Application of the NorSand Model ↗

issmge.org · NUMGE 2023

Covers the 3D formulation and calibration workflow, including guidance from Jefferies & Shuttle (2005) on fitting parameters to lab data.

Jefferies M (2022), "On the fundamental nature of the state parameter" ↗

Geotechnique 72(12), 1082–1091

Theoretical grounding for the state parameter ψ. Argues that ψ's control over soil strength isn't just an empirical correlation but a formal mathematical consequence of Casagrande's classic void-ratio/strength framework

Shuttle, Dawn & Jefferies, Mike. (2005). NORSAND: DESCRIPTION, CALIBRATION, VALIDATION AND APPLICATIONS. ↗

A reference text for engineers. It explains the model’s limits, methods for calibrating its parameters from tests, and validates its performance before demonstrating applications in numerical/finite-element analyses

Software implementations

NorSand as implemented in commercial geotechnical software.

GeoStudio SIGMA/W ↗

seequent

SIGMA/W is the finite-element stress/deformation analysis program in GeoStudio. It uses NorSand to calculate how the soil responds as loads or boundary conditions change

FLAC3D ↗

Itasca

FLAC3D is a 3D finite-difference method program that uses NorSand, among many other models, to simulate stress-strain behaviours in 3D

RS2 ↗

Rocscience

RS2 is a finite-element stress/deformation program that analyses stability in rocks and soils. It added NorSand in 2019 to better model liquefaction-related behavior.
