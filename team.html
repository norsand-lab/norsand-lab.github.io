Our Team

NorSand Lab is built by a small team from McGill and McMaster University — working together to make critical state soil mechanics easier to teach, learn, and verify.

Dr. Fangzhou (Albert) Liu

McGill Associate Professor

A core focus of Liu's research has been the study and analysis of natural and human-induced disasters. Liu has co-organized numerous workshops and conferences on the analyses of geomaterials and on the post-earthquake impact of geohazards.

Dr. Ekansh Agarwal

McGill Postdoctoral Fellow

Ekansh Agarwal is a Postdoctoral Fellow in Civil and Environmental Engineering at the University of Alberta, with a PhD from RMIT University/CBRI-India and a Master's from Delhi Technological University. His research spans tailings stability, granular-fluid simulations, and AI applications in geomechanics.
